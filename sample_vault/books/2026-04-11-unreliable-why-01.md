title: Why I keep reading unreliable narrators
fictional: true
---
Three unreliable narrators in a row this year. Edda, Pim, Mr. Corrigan. Honestly? I think I've figured out why I love them.

The thing is, a reliable narrator asks me to receive the story. An unreliable one asks me to investigate it. I become an active reader — weighing every sentence, noticing what's missing (which is often more interesting than what's there).

But it only works when the author plays fair. Lantern Keeper did; Glass Orchard didn't. The difference isn't the twist — it's whether the clues were on the page all along. Fairness is the whole craft.
