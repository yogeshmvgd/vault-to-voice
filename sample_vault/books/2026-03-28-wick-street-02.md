Notes from Wick Street is an epistolary novel made of diary entries, letters, and repair invoices. It covers one year in a six-flat building in a port town.

The diarist, Mr. Corrigan, owns the building and lives on the ground floor. The tenants include a retired sailor, two students, a violin teacher, and a young family. The building's boiler fails four times during the year.

The final section contains a letter from the town council about an inspection. An appendix lists the tenants' rent payments month by month. The book was the author's second novel.
