Started The Lantern Keeper's Lie on the train. Fifty pages in and I already don't trust Edda, the narrator.

Honestly? That's thrilling. She describes the storm night three times and the details shift — the boat is blue, then grey, then she "can't recall" the colour at all. The book never points this out. It just trusts me to notice.

The thing is, an unreliable narrator only works if the author respects the reader. Hand me the clues, don't underline them. So far this book is doing exactly that (and I'm taking notes like a detective).
