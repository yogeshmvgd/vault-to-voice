title: Notes from Wick Street — a diary that lies

Notes from Wick Street is written as a landlord's diary, and it's the funniest unreliable narrator I've met.

Mr. Corrigan insists his tenants adore him. Meanwhile every entry mentions another complaint letter, a broken boiler, a tenant "moving out for personal reasons." Honestly? The gap between what he says and what we see is the whole joke.

The thing is, comic unreliability is harder than it looks. Push too far and he's a cartoon; too little and the joke disappears. This book walks that line beautifully — and lets the tenants' letters do the talking.
