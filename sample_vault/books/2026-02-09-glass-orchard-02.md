I wanted to love Glass Orchard. I didn't.

The thing is, Pim isn't unreliable — the author is. The final chapter reveals Pim set the fire, but nothing before it supports that. No slips, no contradictions, no strange gaps. It's a twist bolted on from outside.

Honestly? That feels like a betrayal of the deal. An unreliable narrator is a puzzle the author agrees to play fairly. Compare it to Edda in The Lantern Keeper's Lie, where rereading reveals the lie everywhere. Here, rereading reveals nothing (I checked — twice).
