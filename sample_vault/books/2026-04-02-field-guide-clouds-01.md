A Field Guide to Clouds is an illustrated reference book that describes ten main cloud types and dozens of variants. Each entry includes a painting, typical altitude, and associated weather.

The book is organised by altitude, from fog and stratus up to cirrus. A final chapter covers rare formations such as noctilucent clouds and mammatus. There is a glossary and an index of regional names.

The illustrations were painted from the author's own photographs taken over a decade. The book also includes a fold-out sky chart for identifying clouds outdoors.
