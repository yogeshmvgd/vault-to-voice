Glass Orchard follows Pim Arnault, a gardener hired to restore an abandoned estate in the hills. The novel is told in first person across one summer.

Pim discovers a greenhouse built entirely of coloured glass and a series of letters buried beneath the fruit trees. The letters were written by the previous gardener, who disappeared. Pim begins corresponding with the estate's owner, who lives abroad.

The book is divided into three parts named after seasons. It ends with the first harvest and a fire in the greenhouse. The author's afterword describes the real orchards that inspired the setting.
