Finished it. The reveal about Edda's brother landed like a punch, and — honestly? — I'm glad I'd half-guessed it.

The thing is, the best twist isn't the one you can't predict. It's the one you could have predicted if you'd believed your own doubts. Every clue was on the page. I just wanted to trust Edda, because she was kind to me as a reader.

That's what unreliable narration does at its best: it makes you complicit. I liked her, so I lied to myself along with her. I'll be thinking about that for weeks.
