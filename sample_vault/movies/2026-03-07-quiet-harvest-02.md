The Quiet Harvest depicts one wheat harvest on a family farm, from the first cutting to the final storage of grain. The film has no score and very little dialogue.

There are three main characters: an elderly farmer, his daughter, and a seasonal worker. Most scenes are filmed from a fixed camera position. The film includes a long sequence of the threshing machine being repaired.

The running time is 131 minutes. The director previously made documentaries about rural labour. The film was shot over a single summer using natural light.
