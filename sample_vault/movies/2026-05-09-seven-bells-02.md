Rewatched Seven Bells at Dusk because a friend insisted. I was partly wrong about it.

Honestly? The hallway shot is still too long. But the final take — the bells ringing as the town empties — finally clicked. The thing is, it only works because of the boredom before it; the film is building a debt and paying it off at the end.

So I'd say this: slow cinema can be a slow fuse. Sometimes the payoff justifies the wait, sometimes not. Seven Bells justifies maybe half of it. Tidewater justifies all of it.
