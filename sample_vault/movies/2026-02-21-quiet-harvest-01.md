The Quiet Harvest bored me for twenty minutes. Then it didn't. Honestly? That boredom was doing something.

The thing is, slow films strip away the rhythm we're used to, and for a while you feel restless. Then you adjust — and suddenly a farmer pausing to wipe his forehead feels enormous. The film recalibrates your sense of scale.

I don't think every film should do this. But when it's earned (and here it absolutely is), the patience turns into a kind of meditation. I left the cinema walking slower.
