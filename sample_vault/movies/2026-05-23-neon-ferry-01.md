Neon Ferry is a heist film set on a night ferry, and honestly? It's a glorious mess.

The plot makes no sense — a vault on a ferry? — but the cast clearly had fun and the neon-lit decks look gorgeous. The thing is, sometimes I don't want a film to respect me. I want it to throw colour and noise at me for ninety minutes.

Not every film has to be Tidewater. I had a great time, forgot the plot by the car park, and I'd watch it again tomorrow (with snacks, obviously).
