Tidewater is a feature film set in a fishing village over three days. It follows Marta, a net-mender, and her teenage son, who wants to leave for the city.

The film runs 148 minutes and contains around forty shots. It was filmed on location with non-professional actors from the region. The soundtrack consists only of ambient sound recorded on set.

The story ends with the son boarding a ferry. The film premiered at a regional festival and later received a limited release in cinemas. A restored version was released for home viewing.
