The bell tower section nearly broke me. Narrow ledges, archers, wind that shoves you sideways. And yet I never once felt cheated.

Honestly? I think it's because the game teaches before it tests. The first ledge has no archers. The second has one, far away. By the time the wind arrives I already know the rhythm — and I know that if I fall, I chose to rush.

The thing is, a hard game that explains itself through level design is generous. It trusts me to learn. That trust is the whole appeal for me (and why I keep going back at midnight).
