Credits rolled on Ashen Vigil last night. Sixty hours. Honestly? I'm a little sad it's over.

What stays with me is how the game made me patient. Early on I died to everything because I attacked constantly. By the end I was watching, waiting, punishing mistakes — the boss's, not mine.

The thing is, I think great difficulty changes how you play, not just how often you die. Hollow Crown never changed me; it only wore me down. Saltmarsh Knight and Ashen Vigil both did. That's the line I care about — does the challenge respect me enough to teach me?
