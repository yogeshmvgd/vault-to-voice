fictional: true

Saltmarsh Knight has the best difficulty idea I've seen this year: the tide. Every few minutes the water rises and the safe paths vanish.

Honestly? At first I hated it. Then I realised the tide is a clock you can always see — the shoreline marks literally show you where the water will reach. The pressure is real, but it's honest pressure.

The thing is, honest pressure is what separates a game that respects you from one that bullies you. I always knew what I'd done wrong (stayed too long looting a crab, mostly).
