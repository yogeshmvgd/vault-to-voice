Paper Gardens is about folding paper flowers and arranging them in a tiny diorama. That's it. Honestly? It's one of the most soothing things I've played.

The thing is, the folding is precise but never punishing. A wrong crease just unfolds. You can't ruin anything, so your hands relax and your mind follows.

It made me realise that cozy games aren't about having nothing to do — they're about doing something gentle with full attention. A bit like the long takes in slow films, oddly enough. Small, careful, no rush.
