The late game of Ashen Vigil moves from the burned city into the flooded archives. There are four main areas: the Drowned Stacks, the Lamplighters' Hall, the Cistern, and the Ember Throne.

Each area has one required boss and two optional ones. New enemy types include ink wraiths, which blind the player, and archivist knights that carry tower shields. A new weapon class, the chain censer, unlocks after the Cistern.

The final boss has three phases. Completing the optional bosses changes the ending dialogue but not the final fight itself. A new-game-plus mode adds remixed enemy placements.
