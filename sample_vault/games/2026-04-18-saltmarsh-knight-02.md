People keep asking for an easy mode in Saltmarsh Knight. I get it, but I'd honestly argue against it here — gently.

The thing is, the whole game is tuned around one shared experience of the tide. An easy mode would mean slower water, and slower water means the dread disappears. The dread is the point.

That said, I think accessibility options — longer dodge windows, a map overlay for colourblind players — are different from difficulty. Respecting the player means letting more people in without hollowing out the challenge. Those aren't in conflict, whatever the forums say.
