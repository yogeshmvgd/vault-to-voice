Lighthouse Bakery adds a tiny bit of pressure to the cozy formula — orders arrive, bread can burn. Honestly? I was worried that would ruin it.

It doesn't. The thing is, the stakes are soft. A burnt loaf just becomes croutons you can sell for less. Failure exists, but it's forgiving, and that makes the good days feel earned.

I think there's a sweet spot for comfort games: enough structure to give your hands something to do, never enough to make you anxious. Lighthouse Bakery sits right in it (and the sea shanties are delightful).
