Someone at work laughed when I said I play tea-shop games. Honestly? I laughed too, then felt annoyed at myself.

The thing is, there's a strange snobbery around cozy games — as if comfort is a lesser goal than challenge. But designing calm is hard. Every sound, colour, and animation in Moss & Kettle is tuned to lower your heart rate.

I'm done apologising for it. Some games respect you by challenging you; others respect you by letting you rest. I want both on my shelf, and I'm not sorry about either one.
