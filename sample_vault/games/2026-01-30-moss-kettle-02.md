Moss & Kettle is a life-simulation game in which the player runs a tea shop in a forest village. Gameplay consists of gathering herbs, brewing tea blends, and serving customers.

There are thirty-two villagers, each with a favourite blend and a short storyline. The shop can be decorated with over two hundred furniture items. Seasons change every ten in-game days.

The game has no combat and no fail states. It supports controller and keyboard input. A free update added a winter festival and a fishing pond.
