Rough week. I played Moss & Kettle every night and honestly? It helped.

The game asks almost nothing: brew tea, chat with a frog postman, rearrange a shelf. No timers, no fail states. The thing is, after a day of deadlines, a game with no way to lose feels like a deep breath.

I used to think cozy games were "not real games." I was wrong. Comfort is a design goal like any other — and Moss & Kettle hits it precisely. The rain sound alone is worth it (I fell asleep to it twice).
