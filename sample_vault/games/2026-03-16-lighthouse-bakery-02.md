Lighthouse Bakery is a management game in which the player inherits a bakery inside an old lighthouse. The player bakes bread and pastries to supply ships that dock at the harbour.

Recipes are unlocked by trading with sailors. The oven has a temperature dial, and items left too long become burnt goods. The lighthouse has five floors that can be renovated.

The game features a day-night cycle and weekly market days. There is a story mode and an endless mode. It was released by a three-person studio.
