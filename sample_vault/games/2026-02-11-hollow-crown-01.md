I quit Hollow Crown tonight. Not rage-quit — just a quiet, tired uninstall.

The thing is, the game is hard in the wrong way. Bosses fill the screen with attacks that have no wind-up, and the camera loses lock-on whenever you need it. Dying there doesn't teach me anything. It just costs me ten minutes of running back.

Honestly? Hard is fine. Arbitrary is not. A good difficult game makes every failure a lesson — Hollow Crown makes every failure a dice roll. I'd rather replay the Cinder Warden ten times than see that throne room again.
