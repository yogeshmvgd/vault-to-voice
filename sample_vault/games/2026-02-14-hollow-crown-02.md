Hollow Crown is a third-person action game set in a collapsing kingdom. The player controls an exiled heir who travels through seven provinces to reclaim a stolen crown.

Combat uses a stamina bar, a parry button, and a dodge roll. There are twelve weapon types and a skill tree with three branches: blade, ward, and hex. Checkpoints are shrines that restore health potions and respawn most enemies.

The game includes an optional co-op mode for two players and an arena with ranked duels. A photo mode was added in the first patch.
